Work From Home Reimbursement
Employees working remotely are eligible for a monthly reimbursement of up to $100. This allowance is intended to cover expenses such as internet bills, electricity usage, or co-working space fees. To claim the reimbursement, employees must submit their receipts through the HR portal before the 5th of the following month. Reimbursements will be processed with the monthly payroll. Please note that only actual, itemized expenses will be considered, and claims without valid receipts may be rejected.
The company recognizes that remote work setups vary widely depending on an employee's living situation, household size, and local utility costs. For this reason, the reimbursement cap is reviewed annually by the People Operations team and may be adjusted based on regional cost-of-living data. Employees who work in a hybrid arrangement, splitting time between the office and home, are still eligible for the full allowance as long as they maintain a documented home workspace. Contractors and interns are not covered under this policy unless explicitly stated in their engagement letter. If an employee's internet or electricity bill is shared with roommates or family members, only the employee's proportional share may be claimed, and supporting documentation such as a cost-splitting agreement may be requested by HR during an audit.

Learning & Development Budget
We believe in continuous learning and want to support employees in growing their skills. Every employee is eligible for an annual learning and development allowance of $500. This budget can be used for online courses, certifications, technical books, or workshops that are relevant to your role. To access this benefit, submit your request to your manager for approval before making the purchase. The amount will either be reimbursed or directly covered by the company. Employees are encouraged to share their learnings with the team to maximize its value.
Unused learning budget does not roll over into the following calendar year, so employees are encouraged to plan their learning goals early rather than waiting until the final quarter. Popular uses of this allowance include cloud certification exams, conference tickets, language learning subscriptions, and paid technical newsletters. Managers are expected to review requests within five business days. For expenses above $500, employees may request a one-time exception from their department head, though approval is not guaranteed and is generally reserved for certifications directly tied to a current project. Employees who complete a course or certification using this budget are asked, but not required, to give a short knowledge-sharing session to their team within one month of completion.

Health & Wellness Policy
Employee well-being is a top priority for us. Each employee is entitled to a wellness allowance of up to $200 per calendar year. This can be used toward gym memberships, fitness classes, wellness apps, yoga programs, or other health-related activities. Employees can submit receipts through the HR system to claim reimbursement. In addition to this allowance, the company encourages everyone to take regular breaks, use their annual leave fully, and maintain a healthy work-life balance.
The wellness allowance can be combined across multiple activities in the same year, for example splitting the $200 between a gym membership and a meditation app subscription, as long as total claims stay within the annual cap. Family memberships are only reimbursed up to the equivalent cost of an individual plan. The company also runs quarterly wellness challenges with small prizes, participation in which is entirely optional and has no bearing on performance reviews. Employees experiencing burnout or ongoing stress are encouraged to reach out to the confidential Employee Assistance Program, which is separate from this reimbursement allowance and available at no cost regardless of tenure.

Home-Office Setup
To help employees work comfortably and productively from home, the company provides a one-time home-office setup reimbursement of $300 for all new remote employees. This allowance can be used for items such as a desk, ergonomic chair, monitor, or other essential equipment. The purchase must be made within the first three months of joining, and receipts should be uploaded through the HR portal. Items purchased remain the property of the employee, but it is expected that they are used to support home-office work.
Employees transitioning from an office-based role to a fully remote role due to relocation or a team restructuring are also eligible for this one-time allowance, even if they are not new hires, provided they have not previously claimed it. Equipment purchased under this policy is covered by the standard one-year manufacturer warranty where applicable, and the company does not provide separate insurance for these items. If an employee leaves the company within six months of claiming this reimbursement, they may be asked to return the purchased equipment or repay a prorated portion of the allowance, at the discretion of HR.

Flexible Work Hours
While our general office hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, we recognize that flexibility helps everyone do their best work. Employees are allowed to adjust their schedules to suit personal needs, such as childcare, medical appointments, or other responsibilities, as long as their work commitments and team collaboration are not affected. Managers should be informed of any significant changes to regular hours. Teams are encouraged to set core hours for meetings and collaboration, while allowing flexibility outside of those times.
Core collaboration hours are typically set between 11:00 AM and 3:00 PM local time, during which employees are expected to be reachable for meetings and urgent requests unless otherwise agreed with their manager. Outside of core hours, employees may start earlier, finish later, or split their working day, provided total hours and deliverables remain consistent with their role's expectations. Employees working across different time zones from their immediate team should coordinate with their manager to establish a documented working-hours agreement, which is reviewed every six months or whenever team composition changes significantly.

Pet-Friendly Office Policy
Employees may bring well-behaved dogs to the office on designated Pet Fridays, provided they are up to date on vaccinations and other employees in the workspace have no allergies or objections. Cats and other pets are not currently permitted in the office due to space constraints, though this policy is reviewed annually.
